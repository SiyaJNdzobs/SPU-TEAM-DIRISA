### Video Overview
This video presentation demonstrates the end-to-end machine learning methodology, longitudinal IEC and Stats SA data harmonization across 921 wards in KwaZulu-Natal, model training and evaluation metrics, and the live interactive decision-support dashboard forecasting 2026 ward-level voter turnout and ballot volume.
